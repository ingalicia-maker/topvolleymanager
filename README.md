# Top Volley Manager

I need a webapp (I can use on the laptop but mainly on the phone adding it at the main screen) to manage my volleyball team regarding inviting them for different training sessions and matches. There will be these teams:
Infantil A (Coach Luismi)
Cadete B (Coach Carla)
Cadete A (Coach Nino)
Juvenil A (Coach Charly)
Junior (Coach Charly)
Primera Nacional (Coach Charly)

There will be players that will stay in more than 1 team training and playing, and there are differents coaches per each team.
I need the best way, for training sessions and for mathes, to have each coach the possibility to select a player from other teams then his/her and contact her via whatsapp or create a downloadeble list to send to a whatsapp group
